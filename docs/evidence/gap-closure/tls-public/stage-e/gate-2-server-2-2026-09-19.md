## Elevated control-plane role

At both baseline and final verification, `veridex-server-2` was the etcd
leader and kube-vip lease holder. The rollout therefore required direct
three-member etcd status, private VIP reachability, an independent provider
console, and immediate rollback readiness in addition to the normal node and
application checks.
